Our project is about a shooting game, where you shoot sea creatures, eventually fighting the main villain, a water octopus creature called Dagon from a show called Jujutsu Kaisen. He hates you and wants you dead. After encountering, you can turn into a new character with new abilities, fighting off crabs, turtles, and eventually Dagon himself. Beat Dagon to win the game (he shows up roughly around score 35)

Press space to start playing the game! Use w, a, s, d to move. Press M1 to shoot bubbles wherever your cursor is. Press R to dash, moving forward with some invincibility for a short period of time. Naobito specifically does not have a dash. After you kill the pufferfish, a trident will appear. Press E to throw the trident wherever your cursor is, instantly killing any fish. After you throw it, you must pick it back up to use it.

Nanami
- Unleashes a small slash during m1s
- Press E to try to get it into a small red box -> if you do, then you unleash a massive amount of dmg

Maki
- Press m1 to unleash like a small rotating ball around you that deals dmg
- Press Q to create a small melee attack that hits a lot of enemies
- Right click to unleash a ranged kind of boomerang attack

Naobito (the hardest to use)
- Press Q to time stop, freezing time -> You must hold a certain key and move in that direction and then you get teleported. If you don't, you teleport from where you started the time stop and be stunned for a few seconds.
- Press Q while your cursor is red above an enemy. You will teleport behind them, and then click space to throw them into a wall, killing them. Only applies to crabs. 

Tips:
- while fighting the mass horde of fish, use your dash to move around in a circle and get them all grouped up. Then, use ur trident and kill them all.
- USE UR DASH it helps
- Nanami is probably the easiest to use during the second stage so use that to your best of ur abilities
- Avoid the red indicators, as that is where the enemy is about to attack. 

Cheats:
